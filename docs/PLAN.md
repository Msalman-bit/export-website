# Home page plan (tasteskill 2.5)

**Line 1, the kind:** product (page type `product`, site-kind `other`): a maker of physical goods, sold
business to business. Photographs, type and flat colour; no shader effects, scenes or loops.

**Line 2, the deal:** hero `film` (dealt by `deal.mjs --type product --has photo`) · nav `nav-mega` ·
close `close-type` · footer `footer-columns` · rhythm `dark-band` · buttons `block` · mark `dots` ·
compositions intro `trio`, customisation `split`, quality band `bleed` (built on the container lines after
the checker's container-edge rule) · moments `pinned-chapters` (process) and `picture-develops` (customisation).
Palette: the client's own colours through `start.mjs brand` (white ground, obsidian #080808 ink and actions,
lime #C5F900 as the accent on the obsidian band and the mark): the brief fixes them, and lime only passes
contrast on obsidian, so the light page is monochrome and lime is spent on dark grounds. Pairing: `archivo`
(dealt third for the trade; taken over the first, a warm serif, because the brief asks for firm, condensed
headlines), set at 82% width.

**Voice card:** technical and dry with plain facts (copy-registers 2, borrowing 1). Pairs: exact, not cold;
sure, not loud. Person: "you" for the buyer, "we" for the work. Sentences 8 to 16 words, one longer where it
explains. No humour. House words: inquiry, quotation, sample, bulk order, private label, panel, lot. Never:
virtue words, certification claims, invented figures. Admission: businesses only, minimum order per product.
